## Agile Delivery Fit
[The appointment scheduling workflow and patient intake would benefit most from an iterative agile scrum approach. The intake and scheduling workflow affects the clinic staff and patients across all six clinics. Adding this to the sprints allows for the feedback of the end-user, which in this case is the staff and clients. Getting feedback early on can help adapt to any changes. Data migration is another area that requires stronger advance planning and control because data includes security features and areas of compliance such as HIPPA. ]

### Rationale
[The product owner would be assigned to the product manager, the Scrum master would be assigned to the nova tech project leader, and the developer role would be assigned to the Nova Tech Technical teams and the IT department at RCHN. Since the product manager already focuses on the overall quality and specifications of the product it is only right to view that person as the owner of the product. The Nova Tech project manager as the Scrum makes sense because he or she focuses on the advancements of the overall project. For the developers I chose both the technical team at Nova Tech and the RCHN IT department because those two teams can work together to agree on whether the part of the product or increment works. ]

## Sprint Planning Reflection
[The items that I selected form a well-integrated sprint 1 plan that is centered on clinic user experience. Standardizing the intake fields for all six clinics helps address the data input needed to build the workflow prototype. Adding the legacy data cleanup review allows the team to uncover any data risk early on without having to lose focus on the prototype delivery. These three items fulfill the sprint goal that focuses on the workflow of the clinic end user. Selecting the two highest priority items allowed the team to focus on less all while still leaving room for any unexpected issues. The team may first need to learn how each of the six clinics operate with the system this feedback can help shape the final product that will work for all six clinics.  An item that was deferred was the clinic role permissions matrix. This item fits more in the governance side of the project so it would not have fitted in with this initial sprint. ]

## Scrum Master Reflection
### Obstacle and Response
[An impediment that could affect the progress toward the sprint goal would be deciding what areas of the intake process should be worked in for the new system. If some clinics disagree on one area and other clinics agree on that area to be priority then there will be little to no progress toward the sprint goal and another sprint would have to take place about that certain issue prolonging the progress toward the intake area of the goal. The scrum master can help resolve this issue by coaching the product manager which has the accountability toward the product prioritizing which item and area needs to be looked at first. ]
