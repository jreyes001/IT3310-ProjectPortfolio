## Gap Analysis
### Communication Gap
[Reviewing our matrix against Marcus Webb's delivery standards reveals a potential gap regarding External Vendor Dependencies. While internal team channels are robust, third-party software vendors interacting with the RCHN system lack a formalized status cadence. Fix: We are adding a bi-weekly vendor synchronization check-in managed by the Technical Specialist to capture upstream integration status before it impacts sprints.]

### Silence as a Communication Decision
[During lengthy testing phases or quiet sprint periods, prolonged silence from the NovaTech team can cause anxiety for RCHN stakeholders, leading to mistrust even when work is proceeding perfectly on schedule.
Prevention: Our plan utilizes regular push status emails and shared dashboard views to maintain consistent visibility, ensuring stakeholders never have to guess project status.]
