## Engagement Strategy Reflection
### Highest Priority Stakeholder
[The Highest priority stakeholder would be the RCHN Chief Medical Officer (CMO). The CMO is in the middle of the project making high level decisions and receiving any clinical operation feedback. If overlooked there can be issues with the approval of the new software deployment along a built system that doesn’t provide efficient workflow for the clinics. ]

### Risk of Omission
[I decided not to add the patients which are one of the external stakeholders. This is because of the priority of the stakeholders that are directly involved with the project. I wanted to only add those that have enough influence to get the project moving forward. Even though the patients were excluded they might be added in the future for any test that can help with the ease of the new system. ]
