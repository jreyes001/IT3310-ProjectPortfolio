**Consultant:** Jesus Reyes
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 21, 2026
---
## User Persona
**Name:** Sarah
**Role:** Intake Coordinator
**Years in Role:** 1-3 years
**Tech Comfort Level:** Medium, comfortable with common tools
---
### Primary Work Goal
Register incoming patients efficiently and accurately while scheduling the patients without any errors or reliability issues. 
---
### Main Frustration with the Current System
Navigating through too many screens for the registration process, manually re-entering any data, and no confirmed appointments. 
---
### What Would Make Their Work Better
A sinlge screen system that carries any data forward automatically and provides a succesful appointment confirmation.
---
## Product Vision Statement
For RCHN clinic intake coordinators who struggle with multi-screen navigation, re-entry of data, and unconfirmed appointments, a unified single-screen intake and scheduling system will allow for a consistent and reliable workflow across all sic clinics that eliminates any appointment errors and reduces time lost during patient check-ins. 
---
### Rationale
The intake coordinator is one that I found most affected by the system because this person is at the frontline registering and scheduling new patients. The need to have a single screen system eliminates data processing issues while navigating through pages. The outcome helps with the organization’s workflow efficiency and data integrity.
