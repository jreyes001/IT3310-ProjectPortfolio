# Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Jesus Reyes
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 24, 2026
---
## Scope
### In Scope, Phase One
[Patient Intake workflow standarization across all sic clinics, Appointment scheduling system replacement, staff training for new system at all six locations, initial rollout support for 90 days post launch]

### Out of Scope, Phase One
[Patient-facing online scheduling portal, billing system integration with the new scheduling data, ececutive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership, mobile application for clinic staff]

### Scope Justification
[The one item that I chose to be excluded is the patient facing online scheduling portal. This item should be excluded because it is not part of the daily operations of the six clinics. This is more of an external project. This can affect the scope complexity, adding more to the cost of the project and resources needed. It can also delay the project if there are add-ons to the already established workload. ]
---
## SMART Objectives
### Objective 1
[By the end of the approved 90-day post launch rollout period all front desk and intake staff in all six clinics will execute daily operations using a single screen intake and scheduling system. ]
**Connection to Product Vision:** [This directly aligns with the needs of Sarah the intake coordinator by replacing a multiscreen navigation and repetitive data entry with a unified single screen system that eliminates workflow inconsistency. ]

### Objective 2
[After the 30-day staff training for all six clinics, there will be an error-free intake and scheduling system that eliminates any lost records during the intake process and any double-booked appointments.]
**Connection to Product Vision:** [This fulfills the core promise of the product vision by restoring trust, integrity and the guarantee of successful appointments. ]

---
## Planning Constraints
### Constraint 1
[ Multi-site rollout and training for all six clinics. ]
**Effect on Planning:** [This constraint affects the scope, schedule cost, resources, risk, and quality because training must be divided across all six sites during the 30-day training window and the increased risk of a specific site not having the approved hardware or network for the rollout. ]

### Constraint 2
[The checkpoint of a software with zero lost records and zero double booked appointments before the rollout of the system. ]
**Effect on Planning:** [This constraint affects the scope, schedule cost, resources, risk, and quality because it requires intensive testing on the data persistence and immediate saving confirmation. The checkpoint will act as an approval for deployment which can cause scheduling conflicts if a pause is needed. ]

---
## Assumptions
[Assumption 1 includes if all RCHN locations have the right hardware, software and network that will allow for a seamless deployment of the new system. 
If one or more clinics have outdated hardware or slow internet, the deployment of the new system will be delayed. 
The second assumption is assuming that training for the new system will be quick without any disruptions. 
If the staff cannot attend a training session due to the daily workload, this could delay the training completion. 
]
---
## Planning Foundation Synthesis
[One planning decision was excluding the patient online and billing integration from phase one of the project. This scope decision reduces any technical risk, system dependencies, and ensures that resource allocation remains on the initial problem of the intake and schedule replacement for the new system. ]
