# Product Backlog: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** [Jesus Reyes]
**Role:** Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Regional Care Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** [10/20/2026]
---
## Product Backlog

### Story 1
**User Story:** As a [Intake Coordinator], I want to register new patients using a unified single-screen interface so that carries input data forward automatically so that I can eliminate multiscreen navigation and data re-entry for all six clinics. 
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given the intake coordinator is on the patient registration form when data is typed in the contact fields, then all demographic and contact fields are contained within a single screen layout. 
**Story Points:** [13 pts]
**MoSCoW Priority:** [Must Have]

### Story 2
**User Story:** As a Front Desk staff Member, I want to receive an immediate confirmation message upon successfully saving a patient’s appointment, So that I no longer have to double book appointments. 
**Acceptance Criteria:** [Given an appointment form is completed and submitted, when the form is processed by the system, then a pop-up confirmation notice appears on the screen. ]
**Story Points:** [8 pts]
**MoSCoW Priority:** [Must Have]

### Story 3
**User Story:** As an intake coordinator, I want to complete a standardized digital onboarding workflow during daily operations, so that the patient intake process are consistent for all six clinics.
**Acceptance Criteria:** [Given staff members are operating in all six clinics, when they open the intake form, then they can access the identical field layout.  ]
**Story Points:** [5 pts]
**MoSCoW Priority:** [Should have]

### Story 4
**User Story:** As a Clinic Manager, I want to be able to access a reporting dashboard to track operational metrics and patient volume, so that I can evaluate staff performance and clinic efficiency when the new system is implemented. 
**Acceptance Criteria:** [Given an executive user is viewing the reporting dashboard, when they apply filters by date range or clinic location, then the dashboard allows filtering reports accordingly.]
**Story Points:** [8 pts]
**MoSCoW Priority:** [Could Have]

### Story 5
**User Story:**  As a patient I want to schedule my appointments online through a self-service web portal, so that I can book visits independently without calling the clinic.
**Acceptance Criteria:** [Given a patient navigates to the external web portal, when they view open appointments, then they can schedule their appointments.]
**Story Points:** [3 pts]
**MoSCoW Priority:** [Won't have this time]
---
## Prioritization Rationale
### Must Have Justification
[Story one was a must for me because this story directly addresses one of the main issues that the six clinics are having. The story addresses the problem and the solution of turning a multi-screen system into a single screen. It also adds the concern of data being carried over automatically which also solves the re-entry of data problem. ]

### Won't Have Rationale
[User Story 5 was a wont have because it doesn’t address or fix any problem or is even in scope that aligns with the goals. The story addresses a want rather than a need and falls outside of internal operations for all six clinics. ]
---
## Backlog Reflection
### Persona Connection
[User story 1 is supported by week 3 Persona Sarah the Intake Coordinator. Sarah’s main frustration with the old system included using multiple screens to register patients, data re-entry, and no confirmation on appointments saved.]

### Scope Connection
[The week 5 Scope established “patient intake workflow standardization for all six clinics as in scope item for Phase one. User Story 3 translates this requirement into detail for developers. It adds the acceptance criteria around the standardized interface along with field validation rules before any submission is done.]
